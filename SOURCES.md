**Plate names (short forms).** No documented short form of a Togo code-area name was found, so the `KNOWN` table in
`tools/name_ranges.py` is empty and every short plate name comes from the rule itself (engine/SPEC.md 3.2) or waits for
the commune. Inputs: area names from OpenStreetMap [44] and canton names from HDX COD-AB [41]; préfecture chefs-lieux
as listed in `tools/name_ranges.py` (`SEATS`, to check against
the official list); the meaning of kopé, kondji, kodzi and kpota (village, outskirts) is common Ewe and Mina usage, not
taken from a published gazetteer. Any short form added later is cited here and in `KNOWN`.

The published literature the paper cites (addressing standards, check digits, the Hilbert curve, building-footprint
datasets, emergency-care and delivery studies) is listed with full bibliographic details in
[docs/paper/references.bib](docs/paper/references.bib).
