## How to credit N-Ways

Copy the line that fits. Link it to https://github.com/n-ways/open-addresses-togo where you can.

**An app, a website or an API that shows, decodes or issues these addresses:**

Put it next to the address, or on the screen that shows it, and in your about or licences screen. If you ship or change the engine, also keep [NOTICE](NOTICE) (Apache 2.0, section 4(d)).

## Sources inside the data

The data is built from open sources (full list in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md)). Their own terms travel with it, and the data credit above names them:

| Source | Licence | What you owe it |
|---|---|---|
| OpenStreetMap (buildings, places, gates and enclosures derived from it) | ODbL 1.0 | "© OpenStreetMap contributors"; share-alike, which is why the data is ODbL |
| Microsoft building footprints, via Overture Maps | ODbL 1.0 as distributed by Overture (Microsoft releases them under CDLA Permissive 2.0) | credit Microsoft and the Overture Maps Foundation |
| Google Open Buildings, via Overture Maps | CC BY 4.0 | credit Google and the Overture Maps Foundation; compatible with redistribution inside an ODbL database |
| OpenStreetMap: the national boundary, the 69 quartiers of the former commune of Lomé, its named streets and the place nodes the code areas are named after | ODbL 1.0 | "© OpenStreetMap contributors" |
| Canton boundaries (HDX COD-AB Togo, admin3) | CC BY-IGO 3.0 | credit OCHA and the source on HDX |
| N-Ways places (used only to classify buildings: OSM, Overture, Foursquare OS Places, All The Places) | per source (ODbL, CDLA-Permissive-2.0, Apache-2.0, CC0) | only categories are used, no place records are redistributed |

**Postcodes.** Togo has no postcode; none is in this repository. P.O. boxes of the Société des Postes du Togo are not addresses and are not in it either.

**Official addresses.** In Grand Lomé the District's addressing (DAGL) is official: in the former commune of Lomé the official street is shown first, and a DAGL address, once known for a gate, replaces it (engine/SPEC.md section 12). The State's data keeps its own terms and is not redistributed here. Both licences above let the State relicense or republish the registry if it adopts it.

**Personal data.** The registry holds places, never people (docs/paper.md, Privacy).
